Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 October 2026

Township Connect respects your privacy and is committed to being transparent about how information is collected, used and protected when you use the Township Connect application.

This Privacy Policy explains the information Township Connect may collect, why it is collected, how it is used, how it may be shared, and the choices available to you.

You can browse Township Connect and discover businesses without creating an account. Some features require an account.

1. About Township Connect

Township Connect is a community-focused business discovery platform designed to help people discover, explore, save, review and interact with businesses in townships.

The platform also allows businesses and community members to contribute business information and helps users discover local businesses, deals, promotions and services.

2. Information We Collect

Township Connect collects information needed to operate the application, provide its features, maintain accounts, improve the service and understand community interests.

2.1 Account information

When you create an account, Township Connect may process information necessary to create and maintain your account.

This may include:

  • Email address
  • User account identifier
  • Name or other profile information that you choose to provide
  • Phone number, where you choose to provide one
  • Township or other profile information that you choose to provide
  • Profile photo, where you choose to provide one

Township Connect does not require users to provide their home address simply to browse the application.

2.2 Optional age, gender and ethnicity information

Township Connect may allow users to voluntarily provide demographic information in their profile.

This may include:

  • Age or date of birth
  • Gender
  • Ethnic group

Providing this information is optional. Where the application gives you the option to skip a field or select "Prefer not to say", you can choose not to provide the information.

Optional demographic information is not required to browse Township Connect and use the core business-discovery experience.

Demographic information may be used in combined and appropriately de-identified analysis and reports to help Township Connect understand broad community preferences and improve the platform.

Demographic information is not displayed publicly on your reviews or intentionally displayed to businesses as part of your public profile.

You can change or clear optional demographic information through the profile controls provided by the application.

2.3 Business preferences and interactions

Township Connect may collect information about how users interact with businesses and business categories within the application.

This may include:

  • Businesses you save
  • Businesses or categories you select
  • Businesses you view
  • Categories or townships you search
  • Ratings and reviews you submit
  • Interactions with business listings
  • Interactions with deals or promotions
  • Other preferences or selections you make within the application

This information helps Township Connect provide features such as saved businesses and relevant business discovery.

Aggregated and appropriately de-identified information about these interactions may also be used to understand broader township business trends and community interests.

2.4 Business information

Township Connect may contain information about businesses submitted by business owners, customers or other users.

This may include:

  • Business name
  • Business description
  • Business category
  • Business location or address
  • Business contact details
  • Business website
  • Operating hours
  • Business images
  • Other business-related information submitted to the platform

Business information may be displayed publicly within Township Connect so that other users can discover and support local businesses.

2.5 Reviews and ratings

When you submit a review or rating, the review may be associated with your Township Connect account.

This allows Township Connect to maintain review history, support the integrity of business ratings and operate community features such as business reviews and Trust Scores.

Users should avoid including unnecessary personal or sensitive information about themselves or other people in public reviews.

2.6 Location information

Township Connect may use your device location when you use features such as Near Me, map functionality or distance-based business discovery.

Location access is controlled through your device permissions. You can deny or withdraw location permission through your device settings.

If you deny location permission, certain location-based features may not function correctly.

2.7 Usage and analytics information

Township Connect provides a privacy setting that allows you to control optional usage-data collection.

When usage-data collection is enabled, Township Connect may record how the application is used, such as searches, categories, townships viewed and businesses viewed.

These usage events are designed not to include your name, email address, phone number, exact address or account ID.

You can turn optional usage-data collection off through:

Privacy & Settings → Data Collection

When optional usage-data collection is turned off, Township Connect does not send optional analytics or usage events from that device.

2.8 Technical and diagnostic information

Township Connect and the third-party services used to operate the application may process technical information necessary to operate, secure and maintain the application.

This may include:

  • Application performance information
  • Crash information
  • Diagnostic information
  • Device and application information
  • Authentication and security information

This information may be used to identify crashes, troubleshoot technical problems, maintain application security and improve application reliability.

3. How We Use Information

Township Connect may use information to:

  • Create and maintain user accounts
  • Authenticate users
  • Provide business discovery and directory features
  • Allow users to save businesses
  • Allow users to submit ratings and reviews
  • Provide maps and location-related functionality
  • Provide deals and promotions
  • Allow users to submit and manage business listings
  • Understand community interests and business trends
  • Improve Township Connect
  • Monitor application performance and resolve crashes
  • Protect the application against misuse and security threats
  • Respond to support requests
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements

4. Aggregated and De-identified Information

Township Connect may analyze information about business interests, selections, interactions and optional demographic information to understand broader community and business trends.

For example, aggregated analysis may help Township Connect understand which categories of businesses are popular within particular communities or how broad preferences vary across demographic groups.

Where information is used for these analytical purposes, Township Connect aims to aggregate or de-identify information so that reports and insights do not unnecessarily identify individual users.

Township Connect does not intend to publish individual users' private profile information as business-trend reports.

5. Sharing of Information

Township Connect does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that help Township Connect operate its services, including services for authentication, databases, storage, analytics, crash reporting, notifications and other technical infrastructure.

These service providers may process information on behalf of Township Connect to provide their services.

Information that you intentionally submit as public business information, ratings or reviews may be visible to other users of Township Connect.

Township Connect may also disclose information where required by applicable law or legal process, or where reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, security or property of Township Connect, its users or others.

6. Firebase and Other Service Providers

Township Connect uses third-party services to provide and maintain parts of the application.

These services include Firebase products used as part of Township Connect's application infrastructure, which may include:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Cloud Firestore
  • Firebase Storage
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging

These services may process information necessary to provide their respective functionality.

7. WhatsApp and External Services

When you choose to contact a business through WhatsApp from Township Connect, the application may open WhatsApp with a message prepared for you.

Township Connect does not read or store the contents of your WhatsApp conversations.

WhatsApp is a separate service and is governed by its own privacy policy and terms.

8. Support Requests

Messages submitted through Help & Support may be stored as support requests so that Township Connect administrators can review, respond to and manage the request.

Users should avoid sending unnecessary sensitive personal information through support requests.

9. Data Security

Township Connect uses reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

Township Connect uses authentication, database security rules, access controls and other security measures appropriate to the services used by the application.

However, no internet-connected application or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data Retention

Township Connect retains information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the application's services, maintain legitimate business and security functions, comply with applicable legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce applicable agreements.

Where information is no longer required for these purposes, Township Connect will seek to delete or appropriately de-identify it in accordance with applicable requirements and operational procedures.

11. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their Township Connect account and associated account information.

To request account deletion, use the following path within the app:

Profile → Privacy → Delete your account → Open Support

You can then submit an account-deletion request through Township Connect Help & Support.

Township Connect will review and process account-deletion requests in accordance with applicable legal requirements and its legitimate data-retention obligations.

Certain information may need to be retained where required by law, necessary for legitimate security or fraud-prevention purposes, or otherwise permitted by applicable law.

Where information is retained for such purposes, it will not be retained longer than reasonably necessary for that purpose.

12. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on the feature involved, users may be able to:

  • Browse businesses without creating an account
  • Choose whether to provide optional demographic information
  • Change or clear optional demographic information
  • Control optional usage-data collection
  • Control device location permissions
  • Control notification permissions
  • Edit certain profile information
  • Request account deletion

13. Children's Privacy

Township Connect is not designed specifically as a children's service.

Township Connect does not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If we become aware that information has been collected from a child in circumstances where it should not have been collected, we will take appropriate steps to address the situation.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Township Connect may update this Privacy Policy when the application, its services or applicable privacy requirements change.

When material changes are made, the updated policy will be made available through Township Connect and/or this webpage.

The effective date shown at the beginning of this policy indicates when the policy was last updated.

15. Contact Township Connect

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, privacy practices, your personal information or an account-deletion request, contact Township Connect.
