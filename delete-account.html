Request Account Deletion

If you would like to delete your Township Connect account and associated account data, you can submit a deletion request using the button below.

Important: Account deletion is a permanent action. Once your request has been reviewed and processed, your Township Connect account and eligible associated data will be deleted.
Request Account Deletion

What happens after you submit a request?

Your request will be reviewed by Township Connect. We may need to verify that the request relates to your account before processing the deletion.

Once the request has been verified and processed, Township Connect will delete the account and associated personal data that is eligible for deletion.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law, necessary for legitimate security or fraud-prevention purposes, or otherwise permitted by applicable law.

Information that may be associated with your account

Depending on how you have used Township Connect, your account may have associated information such as:

  • Email address
  • Name or profile information you provided
  • Phone number you provided
  • Township or other profile information
  • Optional demographic information
  • Saved businesses
  • Reviews and ratings
  • Support requests
  • Other account-related information

Need help?

If you have questions about account deletion or your privacy, contact Township Connect.
